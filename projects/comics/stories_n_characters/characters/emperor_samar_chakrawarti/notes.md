## THEMATIC ELEMENTS

1. **Evolution vs. Preservation**
   - Should humanity evolve or stay natural?

2. **Science vs. Spirituality**
   - Can magic and technology coexist?

3. **Love vs. Ambition**
   - What is the cost of progress?

4. **Creation vs. Responsibility**
   - Does he control his creations or do they control him?

## DESIGN NOTES

### Visual Design
- Cybernetic implants on right arm and temple
- Glowing arcane symbols on armor
- Traditional Indian elements fused with futuristic tech

### Signature Symbols
- **Chakrawarti Symbol:** A spinning wheel with a cybernetic core
- **Aegis Logo:** Shield with a glowing eye

### Costume Colors
- **Primary:** Gold and Deep Blue
- **Accents:** Glowing Cyan (tech) and Orange (magic)
- **Theme:** Indian royalty + Cyberpunk

---

## TONE & VOICE

- **Dialogue Style:** Philosophical, confident, sometimes haunting
- **Narrative Voice:** Mythic and epic, with modern sci-fi edge
- **Theme:** "Progress comes at a price"
