# ShraddhuOID Body

## Humanoid Physical Structure

The Body module defines the mechanical and physical architecture of ShraddhuOID.

It is responsible for creating a human-like robotic structure capable of movement, interaction, and hardware integration.

---

# Vision

Develop a modular humanoid body platform with:

- Human-like proportions
- Flexible movement
- Stable balance
- Replaceable components
- Safe human interaction

---


---

# Modules

## Mechanical Structure

Contains the physical framework.

Components:

- Robot frame
- Chassis
- Torso
- Arms
- Legs
- Head structure

---

## Joints

Responsible for human-like movement.

Includes:

- Shoulder joints
- Elbow joints
- Wrist joints
- Hip joints
- Knee joints
- Ankle joints

---

## Actuators

Convert electrical signals into movement.

Components:

- Servo motors
- BLDC motors
- Linear actuators
- Motor controllers

---

## Sensors Integration

Body feedback systems:

- Joint position sensors
- Force sensors
- Temperature sensors
- IMU sensors

---

## Hands

Human interaction system.

Features:

- Gripping
- Object manipulation
- Force control
- Fine movement

---

## Power System

Provides energy to the robot.

Includes:

- Battery system
- Power distribution
- Charging system
- Safety management
